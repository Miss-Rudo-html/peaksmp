What we're about

Survival

Peak Smp is a survival server built around hanging out, building together, and earning everything with in-game effort — never your wallet.

🗝️

Dungeons & Crate Keys

Run our dungeons to earn crate keys and unlock rewards. Skill in, loot out.

🏅

Buyable Ranks

Ranks are bought with in-game money you earn by playing — grind, trade, and climb.

💗

Never Real Money

The big one: we never charge real money. Everything on Peak is earned in-game, for everyone, always.
